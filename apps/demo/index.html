Canvas Physics

Spawn

You own what you spawn. Only the owner may move or delete it.

Controls

Drag on the canvas or use WASD to move. Touch the rocket to arm it. Press P, or use the button, to disable your avatar. A disabled avatar stays in place and no physics act on it. Turn on edit mode, then drag an item you own; the gold ghost previews its placement.

Edges and terrain

Leave by the top and you come back at the bottom at once. Leave by a side and you are out of the scene for two seconds, then you return to the centre. You walk through the hill and the launch pad. The floor holds you. An item still lands on all three.
